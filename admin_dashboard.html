🗣️ Voice Health Tips

Maintaining Healthy Voice
  • Stay hydrated. Drink plenty of water throughout the day.
  • Avoid screaming, shouting, or straining your voice.
  • Rest your voice. Take breaks from talking, especially if you have a cold.
  • Avoid smoking and excessive alcohol consumption.
  • Use a humidifier in dry environments to keep your vocal cords moist.
Warning Signs of Voice Problems
  • Hoarseness or voice changes lasting more than 2 weeks
  • Loss of voice or vocal fatigue
  • Pain when speaking or swallowing
  • Difficulty breathing or shortness of breath
  • Persistent coughing or throat clearing
When to Seek Medical Help
Healthy Voice

Clear, strong voice with no pain or discomfort

Seek Medical Help

Persistent problems, pain, breathing issues
