General Voice Care
Stay Hydrated

Drink plenty of water throughout the day to keep your vocal cords moist.

Avoid Strain

Avoid screaming, shouting, or straining your voice.

Rest Your Voice

Take breaks from talking, especially if you have a cold.

Avoid Irritants

Avoid smoking and excessive alcohol consumption.

Use Humidifier

Use a humidifier in dry environments to keep your vocal cords moist.

Healthy Diet

Eat foods rich in vitamins A, C, and E for vocal health.

Warning Signs of Voice Problems
When to Seek Medical Help
  • Hoarseness lasting more than 2 weeks
  • Loss of voice or vocal fatigue
  • Pain when speaking or swallowing
  • Difficulty breathing
  • Persistent coughing or throat clearing
Professional Help

If you experience any of these symptoms, consider consulting with:

  • ENT (Ear, Nose, and Throat) specialist
  • Speech-language pathologist
  • Vocal coach or therapist
